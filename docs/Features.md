# Features & Mechanics

**Create Shared** expands upon the mechanics provided by the Create mod, adding cooperative tools, shared kinetic components, and seamless multiplayer integration.

---

## ⚙️ Core Mechanics

- **Kinetic Interoperability**: Fully interacts with Create's rotational power network, rotational speed controller, stress units (SU), and transmission components.
- **Multiplayer Sharing**: Designed to facilitate collaboration on servers, allowing teams and players to share rotational resources, logistics, and automated contraptions effortlessly.
- **Ponder Integration**: In-game animated visual scenes powered by Ponder to guide you step-by-step through any complex mechanics.
